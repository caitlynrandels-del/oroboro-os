# Avalanche vs Devils (28 Oct 2026) — Neat Expression

You turned one Avalanche–Devils game at Ball Arena into a living map made of simple pieces that already exist in the Oroboro system.

- Two real seats (Section 140 close to the ice, Section 342 higher up) become fixed observer points.
- Player movements become wireframe skeletons sitting inside 16×16 slime-chunk boxes on the rink.
- Body movements, voice, stillness, the crowd of 18,101, lights/screens, and song tempos become separate layers that rise and fall.
- Real NHL numbers (5-2 win, goals by Kadri, Kelly, MacKinnon, Schwartz, Brown) and mild dry Denver weather (66°/40°, no rain) give those layers actual times and values.
- Temporal velocity shows how fast any piece is changing and whether the changes line up.

The early third period is the clearest moment: the goals, the crowd, the lights, the closer seat’s view, and the higher-tempo songs all peak in the same short window.

It is not a claim of control. It is a clean visual way to see how many different signals can line up at once inside one arena, using only existing Oroboro components.
